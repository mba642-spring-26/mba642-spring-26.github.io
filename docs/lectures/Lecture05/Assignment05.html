2 About the Dataset

Hospital Quality Improvement Study

For this assignment, we will use data from a hospital quality improvement initiative. The dataset contains records from 40 patients who were readmitted across two departments, tracking clinical outcomes and the impact of a Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) program on patient anxiety.

2.1 Dataset Background

This dataset simulates a quality improvement study at a multi-department hospital system. It captures:

  • Readmission outcomes: How quickly readmitted patients returned after discharge (compared to the 30-day national benchmark)
  • Departmental costs: Treatment costs across Cardiology and Orthopedics
  • Intervention effectiveness: Patient anxiety scores measured before and after an MBSR program integrating guided meditation, breathing exercises, and progressive relaxation

This type of data is commonly used by healthcare managers to evaluate clinical benchmarks, compare departmental performance, and assess the effectiveness of patient wellness programs.
