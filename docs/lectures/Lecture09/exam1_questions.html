2 About the Dataset

2.1 Crestview Medical Center Quality Improvement Study

Crestview Medical Center is a 250-bed community hospital that launched a system-wide quality improvement (QI) initiative to evaluate patient outcomes, operational efficiency, and care delivery models. The hospital collected data on 150 patients across four clinical departments over a 6-month period. Two care delivery models were compared: the existing Standard pathway and a newly piloted Coordinated pathway featuring multidisciplinary care teams.

As a healthcare analyst, you are asked to analyze this dataset to provide actionable insights to hospital leadership.

2.3 Data Dictionary

Variable Type Description
patient_id Integer Unique patient identifier
age Continuous Patient age in years
department Categorical Clinical department: Cardiology, Neurology, Oncology, Primary Care
insurance Categorical Insurance type: Commercial, Medicare, Medicaid
care_pathway Categorical Care delivery model: Standard, Coordinated
los_days Continuous Length of stay (days)
satisfaction Continuous Patient satisfaction score (1-100)
total_charges Continuous Total hospital charges (USD)
readmitted Categorical 30-day readmission: Yes or No
pain_admission Continuous Pain score at admission (0-10)
pain_24hr Continuous Pain score at 24 hours after admission (0-10)
pain_discharge Continuous Pain score at discharge (0-10)
staffing_ratio Continuous Nurse-to-patient ratio during stay
acuity Integer Patient acuity level (1 = low, 2 = mild, 3 = moderate, 4 = severe, 5 = critical)
wait_minutes Continuous Time from check-in to initial assessment (minutes)
