1.1 Learning Objectives

This assignment assesses your ability to:

  • Choose the appropriate ANOVA design for different research scenarios
  • Conduct one-way, repeated-measures, and two-way ANOVA analyses in R
  • Interpret ANOVA tables, interaction effects, and post-hoc comparisons
  • Draw data-driven conclusions in a healthcare management context
