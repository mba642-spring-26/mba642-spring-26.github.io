4.1 Task 1: Rating-Based Conjoint: Urgent Care Center Design (4 points)

A healthcare system is planning to open a new urgent care center and wants to understand patient preferences. They conducted a conjoint survey where 120 respondents each rated 16 hypothetical urgent care profiles (full factorial design: 2×2×2×2) on a 1-10 scale.

The study examines four attributes:

Attribute Level 1 Level 2 Description
Wait Time 15 min 45 min Expected wait before being seen
Hours Extended (7am-11pm) Standard (9am-5pm) Operating hours
Provider Type Physician (MD) Nurse Practitioner (NP) Who provides care
Visit Cost $75 $150 Out-of-pocket cost per visit
